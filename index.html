About Ndi's Nail Bar

Ndi’s Nail Bar is a boutique nail studio founded on the belief that nail care is a form of personal expression and wellness. Every detail, from our rose-gold workspace to our medical-grade sanitation, is designed to offer a tranquil escape.

Founder Ndi believes in sculptured elegance, pairing premium, non-toxic products with masterfully detailed, hand-painted art to make your design dreams a reality. We are dedicated to creating healthy, strong, and stunning nails that make a statement.
